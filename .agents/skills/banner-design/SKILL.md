# Banner Design Principles

## 1. Gradients and Overlays
- Use vibrant, multi-color linear or radial gradients (e.g. `linear-gradient(135deg, #FF9500 0%, #FF2D55 100%)`).
- Ensure text overlay has a dark semi-transparent tint or text-shadow for high contrast and readability.

## 2. Sliding Hero Carousels
- Slide containers should support touch swiping and desktop drag gestures.
- Use dots navigation indicators that scale or change opacity dynamically when active.

## 3. Visual Decorations
- Place abstract 3D-style icons or large translucent icons on the right side of the card as background decoration (`opacity: 0.8` or `0.2` based on card tint).

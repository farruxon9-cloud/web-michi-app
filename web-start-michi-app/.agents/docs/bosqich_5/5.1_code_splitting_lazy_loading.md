## 📑 Bajarilgan vazifalar va Texnik yechimlar

### 5.1.1 React.lazy() bilan komponentlarni bo'lish
- Loyihadagi eng katta komponentlar dinamik ravishda (`React.lazy()`) yuklanadigan qilindi:
  - `JDMNavigation` (219 KB JSX)
  - `Profile` (374 KB JSX)
  - `VoiceAssistant` (114 KB JSX)
  - `DrivingAcademy` (94 KB JSX)
  - `DriverFeed` (102 KB JSX)
  - `JobDetail` (105 KB JSX)
  - `Dashboard`, `AdminDashboard`, `CompanyHome`, `AssistHeroShowcase`, `ServiceComingSoon`, `NotFound`
- Mock data ma'lumotlari (`MOCK_JOBS` va `MOCK_SCHOOLS`) `src/data/mockJobsAndSchools.js` fayliga ajratildi. Bu boshlang'ich synchronous bundle og'irlashishining oldini oldi.

---

### 5.1.2 Suspense fallback komponenti (`src/components/PageSkeleton.jsx`)
- Dinamik chunk yuklanayotganda foydalanuvchiga ko'rsatiladigan glassmorphic shimmer `PageSkeleton` komponenti yaratildi.
- Rotatsiyalar va modallar ustiga `<Suspense fallback={<PageSkeleton />}>` o'rnatildi.

---

### 5.1.3 Route-based splitting
- React Router ro'yxati (`/home`, `/jobs`, `/academy`, `/profile`, `/navigation`, `/assist`, `/admin`) route darajasida bo mezon bilan chunk'larga bo'lindi. Har bir sahifaga kirilganda faqat o'sha sahifa chunk'i yuklanadi.

---

### 5.1.4 MapLibre GL lazy import
- `maplibre-gl` va `react-map-gl/maplibre` kutubxonalari faqat `JDMNavigation` komponenti ochilganda yuklanadigan qilib ajratildi. Natijada boshlang'ich sahifada 1.2 MB xarita moduli yuklanmaydi.

---

### 5.1.5 Vendor chunk ajratish (`vite.config.js`)
- `vite.config.js` faylida `rollupOptions.output.manualChunks` sozlandi:
  - `vendor-react`: React, ReactDOM, React-Router-DOM
  - `vendor-icons`: Lucide-React
  - `vendor-maps`: MapLibre GL, Leaflet, React-Map-GL
  - `vendor-i18n`: i18next, React-i18next

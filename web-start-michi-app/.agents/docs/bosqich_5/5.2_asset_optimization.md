## 📑 Bajarilgan vazifalar va Texnik yechimlar

### 5.2.1 Rasmlarni siqish (JPEG & PNG siqish)
- `public/images/presets/*.jpg` papkasidagi barcha mashina va yuk mashinasi rasmlari (~550KB – 650KB) sifatini saqlagan holda **60KB – 85KB** oralig'iga siqildi.
  - `fuso_supergreat.jpg`: 567KB → **80KB**
  - `toyota_landcruiser300.jpg`: 525KB → **62KB**
  - `isuzu_giga.jpg`: 633KB → **80KB**
  - `hino_profia.jpg`: 587KB → **85KB**
- `public/` papkasidagi yirik PNG rasmlari adaptiv palitra bilan optimallashtirildi:
  - `logo.png`: 496KB → **38KB**
  - `school.png`: 898KB → **108KB**
  - `truck.png`: 816KB → **100KB**

---

### 5.2.2 SawarabiGothic-Regular optimizatsiyasi
- Yaponiyada rezyume (PDF) yaratish uchun ishlatiladigan `SawarabiGothic-Regular.ttf` (1,858 KB) shrift fayli zamonaviy WOFF2 formatiga (`SawarabiGothic-Regular.woff2`) konvert qilindi va **762 KB** gacha qisqartirildi.
- CSSda `font-display: swap` qo'llandi va `index.html`da font preload berildi.

---

### 5.2.3 SVG Sprite optimizatsiya
- `public/icons.svg` (barcha tarmoq ikonkalari `<symbol>` spritelari) va `public/favicon.svg` fayllari optimallashtirildi.

---

### 5.2.4 Preload muhim resurslar (`index.html`)
- `index.html` faylining `<head>` qismiga eng muhim resurslar uchun `<link rel="preload">` yorliqlari qo'shildi:
  ```html
  <link rel="preconnect" href="https://fonts.googleapis.com">
  <link rel="preconnect" href="https://fonts.gstatic.com" crossorigin>
  <link rel="preload" as="font" type="font/woff2" href="/SawarabiGothic-Regular.woff2" crossorigin="anonymous">
  <link rel="preload" as="image" href="/logo.png" type="image/png">
  ```
